- **data curation** — the pipeline that turns raw crawled text into a training set: extract, filter, deduplicate, classify, scrub, decontaminate, mix (Ch. 08)
- **Common Crawl** — a non-profit monthly crawl of the public web, published as WARC files; the raw source of nearly all open pretraining datasets (Ch. 08)
- **WARC** — the archive format Common Crawl uses; stores full HTTP responses including raw HTML (Ch. 08)
- **text extraction** — turning HTML into main-body text (e.g. with trafilatura), dropping boilerplate (Ch. 08)
- **boilerplate** — navigation menus, footers, cookie notices and other text repeated on every page of a site (Ch. 08)
- **language identification** — assigning each document a language and confidence, e.g. with fastText; documents below a threshold are dropped (Ch. 08)
- **heuristic filter** — a hand-written rule that drops a document for a measurable property (length, symbol ratio, repeated lines), as in the Gopher and C4 rule sets (Ch. 08)
- **deduplication** — removing documents that are copies or near-copies of others (Ch. 08)
- **memorisation** — a model reproducing training text verbatim; grows with how often the text was repeated in training (Ch. 08)
- **train–test overlap** — evaluation items present in the training data; the target of decontamination (Ch. 08)
- **exact deduplication** — dropping documents whose normalised text hashes to a value already seen (Ch. 08)
- **near-duplicate** — a document that differs from another only by a few words, a date or a byline (Ch. 08)
- **shingle** — one of the overlapping n-word windows of a document; the document is represented as its set of shingles (Ch. 08)
- **Jaccard similarity** — |A ∩ B| / |A ∪ B| for two sets; the fraction of shingles two documents share (Ch. 08)
- **signature** — a fixed-length summary of a document (64 MinHash values) from which similarity can be estimated (Ch. 08)
- **MinHash** — for each of k hash functions, the minimum hash over a document's shingles; two signatures agree in a fraction ≈ Jaccard of positions (Ch. 08)
- **locality-sensitive hashing (LSH)** — bucketing signatures by bands so that similar documents collide and only bucket-mates are compared (Ch. 08)
- **band** — a contiguous group of r rows of a MinHash signature; a pair is a candidate if any band matches exactly, P = 1 − (1 − J^r)^b (Ch. 08)
- **quality classifier** — a small model scoring documents for training value, trained on labels from an expensive judge (FineWeb-Edu, DCLM) (Ch. 08)
- **distillation of a labelling function** — labelling a sample with an expensive model, then training a cheap model to imitate the labels and score everything (Ch. 08)
- **PII** — personally identifiable information (e-mails, phone numbers, IDs) scrubbed or replaced with placeholder tokens before training (Ch. 08)
- **decontamination** — removing training documents that overlap with evaluation sets, usually by n-gram overlap (Ch. 08)
- **contamination** — evaluation text present in training data, which inflates benchmark scores (Ch. 08)
- **n-gram overlap** — the decontamination test: any document sharing an n-word window (13 in production) with an eval item is removed (Ch. 08)
- **data mixing** — choosing per-source sampling weights (web, code, maths, books, synthetic) for the final training stream (Ch. 08)
- **up-sampling** — repeating a scarce high-value source (e.g. Wikipedia) several times in the mix (Ch. 08)
- **curriculum** — ordering the data mix over training, e.g. saving the highest-quality data for the final annealing phase (Ch. 08)
- **packing** — concatenating documents into one token stream separated by EOS so no padding is wasted (Ch. 08)
- **EOS token** — the end-of-sequence special token placed between packed documents (Ch. 08)
- **synthetic rephrasing** — having an LLM rewrite low-quality pages or generate Q&A/summaries from high-quality ones to add diverse tokens (Nemotron-CC) (Ch. 08)
- **FineWeb-Edu** — a 1.3T-token subset of FineWeb selected by a classifier trained to imitate Llama-3-70B's 0–5 "educational value" ratings (Ch. 08)
- **DCLM** — DataComp-LM: a 2024 benchmark and dataset whose best recipe keeps the top 10% of Common Crawl by a fastText classifier trained on instruction-style text (Ch. 08)
