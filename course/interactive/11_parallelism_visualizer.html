Communication per training step, per device
Challenge. Fit the 70B model on 8 × 80 GB for training. Try every combination of DP/TP/PP and ZeRO stage. What is the smallest per-device memory you can reach, and does it fit? Then switch to inference mode.
Show answerIt does not fit for training with fp32 Adam states, no matter how you split: 70 B × 16 bytes = 1120 GB of state, and 8 × 80 GB = 640 GB of memory. The best you can do — TP·PP=8 with any DP, or DP=8 with ZeRO-3 — is 140 GB per device (plus activations). Real 70B fine-tunes on 8 GPUs use tricks that change the bytes-per-parameter: 8-bit optimizer states, LoRA (no optimizer states for the frozen base), or offloading to CPU. For inference the picture flips: 2 B/param = 140 GB total, so TP=8 gives 17.5 GB per device, and even 2 × 80 GB (TP=2) would fit the weights.
