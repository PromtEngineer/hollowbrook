Challenge. What does $100 buy at 40% MFU on 8×H100 at $2.50 per GPU-hour? Give the FLOPs, the Chinchilla-optimal N and D, and compare with nanochat's reported d20 run.
Show answer$100 / (8 × $2.50) = 5 GPU-hours of wall-clock → 40 GPU-hours. C = 8 × 989e12 × 0.40 × 5 × 3600 ≈ 5.7 × 1019 FLOPs. Chinchilla: N = √(C/120) ≈ 0.69 B parameters, D = 20N ≈ 13.8 B tokens. nanochat's "$100" d20 model (Oct 2025) is reported as ≈0.56 B parameters on ≈11 B tokens in ≈4 h on 8×H100 — the same ballpark, which is the point: the 6ND rule plus an honest MFU predicts real runs within tens of percent.
