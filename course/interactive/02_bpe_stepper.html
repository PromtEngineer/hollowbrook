Train first.

BPE only merges pairs that are frequent. A word that appears once never gets its own token unless its pieces are common elsewhere; that is why rare words and names are split into several tokens, and why the vocabulary size (Chapter 2) is a trade-off between shorter sequences and a larger embedding table.
